British Columbia and its Beautiful Cities

Vancouver, a major metropolis on the coast of British Columbia, has amazing sights to visit. These include, but are not limited to, Stanley Park, Grandville Island Public Market, and the Capilano Suspension Bridge.

Victoria, the historic capital of British Columbia, is located on Vancouver Island. Victoria's old world charm also included the Fairmount Empress Hotel, Canada's oldest Chinatown, and a famous harbor known for orca and whale-watching tours.
